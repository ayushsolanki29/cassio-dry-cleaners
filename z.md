Yes, local SEO is all about location + service + intent. Here's the full breakdown for a laundry/dry cleaning business:

What keywords to target
"Near me" searches are actually resolved by Google using the user's location — you can't directly target "near me" with a keyword. What wins those results is:

Google Business Profile (most important — this is what shows the map pack)
Location-specific pages on your site
Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across the web
What to put in your site content and metadata:

dry cleaner Watford
laundry service Watford
dry cleaning near Watford
wash and fold Watford
ironing service Watford
dry cleaner [neighbourhood] — e.g. Cassiobury, Croxley Green, Nascot Wood
same day dry cleaning Watford
pickup and delivery laundry Watford
What your site actually needs
1. Title tags and meta descriptions with location Currently yours say "Premium laundry and dry cleaning" — no location. Should be:

"Dry Cleaners Watford | Free Pickup & Delivery — Cassio"

2. An /areas page (you already have the folder) One page per service area — Watford, Cassiobury, Croxley Green, Nascot Wood, Bushey, Rickmansworth etc. Each page targets "dry cleaner [area]" searches. This is the highest-impact thing you're missing.

3. Structured data (JSON-LD) A LocalBusiness schema block tells Google your name, address, phone, hours, and service area directly — this feeds the Knowledge Panel and map results.

4. H1/H2 headings with keywords Your hero says "Expert care for..." — Google can't see that you're in Watford. The heading should mention it at least once.

5. Page-specific metadata Each page (services, how-it-works, about) needs its own title/description with location keywords, not generic ones.
